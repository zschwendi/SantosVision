# Experimental reprojection paths — October 11, 2026

Native implementation of the three reviewed alternatives to one quad per source pixel: a retained source-anchor fullscreen copy, an independent analytic two-eye anchor probe, and a conservative adaptive tile mesh, plus the Swift side of the atomic stereo pair bridge. These reprojection alternatives are **initially off**. With no diagnostic flag or saved adaptive choice enabled, the renderer encodes the legacy draw with the legacy uniforms, present-time anchor and acknowledgement. The app's separate **Reduce frame-copy overhead** choice is initially ON and uses the qualified native depth handoff described in [FROZEN-DEPTH-TRANSFER-20261011.md](FROZEN-DEPTH-TRANSFER-20261011.md). The current source-bound build, parity, simulator and diagnostic timing evidence is recorded in [the qualification record](REPROJECTION-QUALIFICATION-20261011.md). No headset, full-scene matched-stereo or 4K60 result is claimed.

## Flags

| Flag | Default | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |

| `MUGUET_COMPOSITOR_SOURCE_ANCHOR_COPY_ALLOW_SYNTHETIC=1` | off | Simulator only: allow the copy for actual game frames when the retained pose has no ARKit anchor (pose fixture). Proves conversion and plumbing, not display adjustment. |
| `MUGUET_COMPOSITOR_ANCHOR_PROBE=1` | off | Replace game content with a frozen analytic scene rendered into two coherent source eye textures from one retained anchor; present it through the mesh or the copy. `_MODE` = `mesh`, `copy` or `alternate` (default, A/B/A every `_SWITCH_SECONDS`, default 5); `_HOLD_FRAMES` (default 8) controls source age; `_SCALE` (default 1) the source size relative to the viewport; `_MAX_MIB` (default 384) caps the probe's retained texture bytes. |
| `MUGUET_COMPOSITOR_ADAPTIVE_MESH=1` | off / saved app choice | 8x8 tile classification on the GPU; accepted tiles draw the same 32-triangle fan through 96 static indices and 33 unique vertices, every other tile the legacy per-texel strip, through two indirect draws. `MUGUET_COMPOSITOR_ADAPTIVE_TOLERANCE_ULP` (default 16) is the stated plane tolerance. ## What is wired

- **Anchor retention** (`XRHeadTracking.swift`). The compositor already assigns the native head frame; the renderer now hands the actual `DeviceAnchor`, the eye request frame and the per-eye transforms and projections to the same submission, and the cache keys them by the exact consumed `head_frame`. `request_frame` is retained alongside and must match the lease; it is never the anchor key. Synthetic simulator poses are retained with `anchor == nil`. The cache is invalidated on recenter, suspend, an untracked submission after a tracked one, and renderer stop. No anchor is ever constructed from matrices or re-queried from an old timestamp.
- **Copy eligibility** (`GameWorldRenderer.swift`, `GameWorldSourceCopy.swift`). One anchor per drawable, so every view's source must share one head frame and one request; two views require a validated atomic pair. The retained pose must reproduce each eye's `game_from_eye` through the lease's `game_from_stage` within tolerance, and its projection must match up to the forward-Z depth-row flip. The lattice gate requires the viewport extent to equal the source size, an integral viewport origin, the identical projection (hence identical rays and depth range), and the present head-from-eye transform equal to the one retained with the pose within a rigid tolerance of 1e-5 in rotation and 0.1 mm in translation, because a changed eye calibration moves the ray origin under the labelled anchor; anything else uses the mesh with a counted reason. The shader reconstructs depth per pixel from source depth and the two projections, writes sky as 0, discards non-finite or out-of-range depth, and applies the Bayer near fade from the present head's position relative to the source eye while geometry and depth stay source-anchored.
- **Analytic probe** (`GameWorldAnchorProbe.swift`, `GameWorldProbe.metal`). Ground, a wall with a hard depth edge in front of a farther wall, sky as clear depth, and a near panel 0.30 m in front of the head rendered into colour plus R32Float NDC depth per eye with the drawable's own view transforms and projections. Texture sets are leased to every command buffer that reads them and returned on completion; the held set is never a write candidate and the writer set is leased before any eye is encoded. The probe never acquires a native lease, never calls `sv_world_complete_frame`, never emits `.presented`, and times itself in a separate `GameCompositorProbe` log namespace with `source_kind` `analytic` or `synthetic`.
- **Adaptive mesh** (`GameWorldAdaptiveMesh.swift`, `GameWorld.metal`). Classification accepts a tile only when all 64 covered depths and the 10x10 halo are valid and non-clear, all 81 geometric corners qualify under the legacy `qualifiedCornerDepth`, every corner, covered centre and halo centre lies on one affine NDC plane within the tolerance, and no corner is behind the eye, outside the clip depth range or inside the near-fade band. Border and partial tiles always fall back. The fan now uses one centre and 32 perimeter vertices with static indices in the original triangle order; perimeter vertices use the same `continuousCornerDepth` values as fine neighbours, so coarse/fine seams share identical vertices. Two padded 32-byte indirect records hold the indexed fan and unchanged strip fallback; scratch sets are bounded (6 sets, 4 MiB each), leased on acquisition and returned on completion, and counters are read from the shared 64-byte argument copy after completion.
- **Pair bridge** (`GameWorldSourceCopy.swift`). Direct call to the committed `sv_world_acquire_pair` with the imported `SVWorldPair`; the 592-byte layout is checked at runtime. Validation rejects a zero scene epoch, shared or zero leases, swapped eye indices, mixed head or request frames, mixed presentation time, mixed size and differing `game_from_stage`. The bridge and its lease handling are tested, but no full-scene coherent stereo producer qualifies to publish a pair.

## Verification

Root's source-bound checks passed Swift 6 strict typechecking for simulator, device and non-runtime builds, offline Metal compilation for three SDKs, the standalone harness build and CPU selfcheck. The full native suite passed 104 tests with zero failed, ignored or filtered. The unsigned simulator and device apps built; the simulator analytic-probe smoke completed with one synthetic view and no GPU/render/allocation failures. Exact receipts and limits are in [the qualification record](REPROJECTION-QUALIFICATION-20261011.md).

The tracked `scripts/reprojection-harness.swift`, built with `scripts/build-reprojection-harness.sh` and run by `scripts/run-reprojection-gpu-batch.sh`, exercises 31 synthetic fixtures, including both the original non-square and added square-pixel 37×23 border cases. Default/OFF colour, depth and coverage are bit-identical to the frozen original across all 31. The indexed fan's output is bit-identical to the earlier unindexed fan on every fixture. The oracle intersects each plane ray in Double precision per pixel, checks predicted coverage and Bayer fade, rejects non-finite or out-of-range written depth, and checks source colour exactly at scale 1 or against an independent bilinear clamp-to-edge UNORM sample within 1 LSB at other scales. Depth has an unchanged 100 µm absolute metric budget for baseline and candidates, plus a per-pixel bound of eight ULP of expected depth and, under camera motion only, a local-gradient subpixel snapping term. Candidate-relative metric error must stay within the larger of 100 µm and 1.1 times the baseline error. Non-oracle fixtures retain the four-ULP candidate-versus-baseline gate; on oracle fixtures that comparison is diagnostic. Known edge, footprint and Bayer-tie exclusions are declared from fixture geometry, not rendered-depth deltas. Exact identity masks, zero novel coverage or cracks, all-fallback identity and predeclared adaptive tile counts remain gates.

The 31-fixture parity runs pass the common regression and candidate-relative guards but fail overall absolute qualification: the added square-pixel border has 128.047852 µm error in the original, legacy and adaptive outputs, above the unchanged 100 µm budget. Diagnostic timing is separate and has a street-copy five-versus-four-ULP comparison failure; its command timings do not establish game or headset performance.

## Known limits and missing qualification

- The copy path is restricted to identical lattice and projection; a planar footprint reconstruction for mismatched lattices is not implemented, so those cases use the mesh.
- The system's display-time adjustment is not exercised by any harness; only headset runs with captured visual evidence can qualify it. The simulator pose fixture has no anchor.
- Adaptive tolerance of 16 ULP remains experimental. Indexed-fan image parity is established on all 31 synthetic fixtures, while the separate timing runs are diagnostic and the inherited square-border absolute failure remains.
- A captured real game's one-draw stereo geometry transformation passed a CPU-only position/depth proof. It does not cover fragment shading, visibility or the complete deferred and post graph, so no full-scene coherent pair is published. With pairing off or not ready, two-view drawables keep independent alternating eyes and the copy path is ineligible for them.
- Probe texture sets are bounded by count (4) and by an aggregate byte cap (default 384 MiB, `MUGUET_COMPOSITOR_ANCHOR_PROBE_MAX_MIB`); when a set would exceed it the probe halves its own analytic source resolution and logs the change. Game resolution is never touched by the probe.

## Post-GPU repair (same day)

Root's first GPU run (`target/verification/reprojection-implementation-v1/runs/root-final-batch-v1`, immutable) failed 7 of 30 fixtures. A CPU float32 emulation of the corner fit and vertex chain against a Double oracle put the failing vertex math within 3 ULP of truth; the 42 and 352 ULP GPU differences under camera motion led to a rasterizer subpixel-snapping hypothesis. This stage introduced per-pixel Double-oracle and metric-depth checks, explicit mask, fan and seam checks, the square-pixel border fixture, and probe command-completion, generation and pool-lifetime repairs. Subsequent source-bound compilation and GPU reruns are recorded above and in [the qualification record](REPROJECTION-QUALIFICATION-20261011.md).

## Harness repair after GPU batch v2 (same day)

Root's second run (`runs/root-final-batch-v2`, immutable) failed 8 of 30 fixtures with the production shader unchanged and the legacy path exact. Its colour oracle compared source texel bytes where the mesh samples with a linear filter at scale 2 and 16; its depth bound used eight ULP of 0.02 as a global floor against a near-crossing plane at depth near 0.9. The square-pixel 37×23 fixture widens the field of view until one output pixel spans about 30 mm of depth, and its baseline measured about 128 µm error. The following harness repair added independent bilinear clamp-to-edge sampling, a local per-pixel depth bound, finite-depth checks, required adaptive tile-class readback and separate baseline, candidate-absolute and candidate-relative verdicts. It retained the original non-square border fixture and the unchanged 100 µm budget. The later 31-fixture rerun confirmed the square border as an inherited baseline absolute failure, while the candidate-relative and common regression guards passed.
